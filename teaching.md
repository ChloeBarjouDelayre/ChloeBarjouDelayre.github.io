## Teaching

During the course of my PhD, I teached for ~ 80 h across various subjects at the undergraduate level, in the [Université Clermont Auvergne de Physique et Ingénieurie](https://eupi.uca.fr). I teached radioactivity for first years of bachelor, where I taught the basics of radioactivity, including the laws of decay. I also taught Practical lesson of electricity for first year of bachelor.
