title: Filter Calibration
---

For precision cosmology, accurate photometry of the observations is required. Consequently, I worked on creating a realistic model for the ZTF filter. This model, called synthetic filters, takes into account all the optics of the ZTF telescope, the real angle of incidence of the light, and the difference between the two types of CCD in ZTF (single coating and double coating).
