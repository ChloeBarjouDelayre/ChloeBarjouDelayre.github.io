title: H₀ anisotropy
---

The cosmological principle assumes the isotropy of the Universe at large scales. Recent disagreements with the standard model motivate the investigation of possible anisotropies in the Universe. A possible anisotropy could be observed in the Hubble constant: H₀. My thesis work focused on creating a method to measure this anisotropy in the ZTF SN Ia sample. The large footprint and efficient sampling of ZTF allow for a H0 anisotropy measurement with unprecedented precision. I started by focusing on the simplest version of a multipole which can reproduce anisotropy, a dipole. I built a likelihood fit of an H₀ dipole and tested it on realistic simulations of the ZTF sample: the injected dipole is recovered without bias, whatever H₀ is assumed.
