About me

I am a Doctor in observational cosmology. I did my PhD (2023-2026) at the Laboratoire de Physique Clermont Auvergne, Clermont-Ferrand, France. My main area of research focuses on study H0 anisotropy using simulations of type Ia supernovae (SN Ia) from the Zwicky Transient Facility ([ZTF](https://www.ztf.caltech.edu/)) survey. I also contribute of the ZTF filters calibration to perform an accurate photometry and be able to do precision cosmolog.
